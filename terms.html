1. Deposits, rescheduling and refunds

A $200 deposit is required to secure your spot. This deposit is non-refundable. However, you are allowed one reschedule as long as at least 48 hours' notice is provided. Rescheduling requests made with less than 48 hours' notice will result in the loss of your deposit.

  • Deposits are applied to your course total. The remaining balance must be paid in full before your class begins; seats are released if the balance is not received.

2. Attendance and certification

  • Credentials are issued through NCCER's Registry. The NCCER Advanced Rigger credential is valid for five years from the date of issue.

  • Arrive on time. A student who misses a required session may be moved to a later class at Prime Lift's discretion.
