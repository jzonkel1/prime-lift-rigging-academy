How do you want to pay?

Pay the deposit by card

You'll enter your card on Stripe's secure checkout page. Your seat is held the moment the payment goes through, and a receipt is emailed to you.

  • Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover
  • Apple Pay and Google Pay on your phone
  • Balance can be paid online any time before class
Pay with Klarna

Klarna covers your course in full and then splits it into scheduled payments for you, so this option charges the full course price, not the $200 deposit.

  • Choose your payment plan on Klarna
  • Instant decision
  • Your seat is confirmed the moment Klarna approves
Pay with Afterpay

Same as Klarna: Afterpay pays the course in full and breaks it into installments for you, so this option charges the full course price rather than the deposit.

  • Instant decision
  • Installments handled by Afterpay
  • Seat confirmed on approval
In-House Financing, No Credit Check

Didn't qualify for Klarna or Afterpay? We'll set you up directly. Start with as little as $200 down and make payments leading up to your class date.

  • No credit check
  • Payments scheduled before your start date
  • Course begins once the balance is paid in full
