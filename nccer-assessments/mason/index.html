Masons lay brick, block and stone for walls, structures and industrial refractory work.

What the assessment covers

The NCCER Mason assessment covers masonry materials and mortar, layout and leveling, laying units to the line, reinforcing and anchoring, and masonry safety. Pass, and the credential is recorded on the NCCER Registry.

Who tests out

  • Experienced masons who need the NCCER credential on paper
  • Hands whose credential is coming due for renewal
  • Hands whose contractor needs the credential verified before a turnaround
  • Anyone hired on the condition of getting the card
